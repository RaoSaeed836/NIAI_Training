🗺️ What You'll Actually Learn

This isn't just theory dumped on you week after week. It's built like a real pipeline — foundations first, then tools, then models, then how to actually ship them.

Week 1–2 · Foundation

Before touching any AI model, you need the groundwork: linear algebra, calculus, probability & statistics, and core Python (NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn). You'll write real scripts, visualize math concepts, and explore how AI is used across industries through case studies.

Week 3 · AI/ML & Cloud Platforms

Understanding the AI pipeline end-to-end and getting comfortable with cloud ML services and dev tools — because production AI doesn't live on your laptop.

Week 4 · Data Pipeline & Feature Engineering

Data cleaning, transformation, encoding, and building real ETL pipelines using BigQuery ML and Dataflow. This is the unglamorous part nobody talks about — but it's where most real-world projects actually live or die.

Week 5–6 · Basic AI & ML

Supervised learning: regression, classification (SVM, KNN, Naive Bayes), decision trees, ensemble models, and your first neural networks. You'll train models, tune hyperparameters, and compare performance hands-on.

Week 6–7 · Advanced AI & ML

Deep learning gets real here — CNNs, RNNs, transformers, attention models. Plus optimization techniques (regularization, dropout) and cloud-based training with TPU/GPU distribution.

Week 8 · Unsupervised Learning

Clustering (K-Means, DBSCAN) and dimensionality reduction — finding patterns in data without labels.

Week 9 · ML Pipeline & Automation (MLOps)

Learn how AI actually gets shipped: CI/CD, versioning, dataset/model tracking, deployment, and workflow automation with Kubeflow Pipelines and Cloud Composer.

Week 10 · Model Optimization & Deployment

Pruning, quantization, knowledge distillation, and performance monitoring — because a working model isn't enough, it has to run efficiently and stay accurate over time.

Week 11 · Responsible & Explainable AI

Bias detection, fairness indicators, explainability tools (SHAP, LIME, What-If Tool), and ethical AI frameworks. Because building AI responsibly isn't optional anymore.

Week 12 · Generative AI

The basics, the technology, and real applications of the tech everyone's talking about right now.

Weeks 9–12 · 🎓 Capstone Project (runs in parallel)

Pick a real-world problem — medical diagnostics, fraud detection, a recommender system, NLP — and build, deploy, and present an end-to-end ML solution on the cloud. This is where everything comes together.
